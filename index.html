Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will I lose my data?

App data is stored locally on your device by default. Before uninstalling the app or switching devices, please make sure your data is backed up. For help with exporting or restoring data, contact us by email.

Q: Notifications or reminders are not working.

Go to Settings → Notifications → RiKe and make sure notifications are allowed. Also check that reminders are enabled correctly within the app.

Q: How can I report a bug or share feedback?

Email the support address above and, if possible, include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue.

Q: How do I request a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Open Report a Problem on your device or visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a request.
